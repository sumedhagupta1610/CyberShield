📚 Cybersecurity Awareness

Practical guidance to keep yourself safe online.

🎣 Phishing Attacks

Phishing tricks you into revealing credentials or downloading malware by impersonating trusted senders. Warning signs include:

  • Urgent language — "Act now or your account will be closed!"
  • Sender address doesn't match the displayed name
  • Hover shows a link destination different from the text
  • Generic greeting ("Dear Customer") instead of your name
  • Unexpected attachments (PDFs, Office files, ZIPs)

What to do: Never click links in unexpected emails. Go directly to the official website instead.

🔐 Strong, Unique Passwords

A strong password:

  • Is at least 12 characters long
  • Mixes uppercase, lowercase, digits, and symbols
  • Contains no dictionary words or personal info
  • Is unique per account — reuse enables credential stuffing

Use a reputable password manager (Bitwarden, KeePassXC) so you only need to remember one master passphrase.

📱 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)

MFA requires a second proof of identity beyond your password. Even if your password is stolen, the attacker still cannot log in.

  • TOTP apps (Google Authenticator, Authy) — preferred
  • Hardware keys (YubiKey) — strongest option
  • SMS codes — convenient but susceptible to SIM-swap

Enable MFA on email, banking, and social-media accounts first.

🌐 Safe Browsing Habits

  • Look for HTTPS and a padlock before entering sensitive information
  • Keep your browser and OS up to date — patches fix known vulnerabilities
  • Avoid public Wi-Fi for sensitive tasks; use a VPN if needed
  • Install only trusted browser extensions; review permissions
  • Clear cookies and cache periodically
  • Use a privacy-focused DNS (Cloudflare 1.1.1.1 or Google 8.8.8.8)
